# EduAgent: 3-Minute Live Viva Demo Script
**A Step-by-Step Guide for Acing the Practical Demonstration (5 Marks)**

---

## Pre-Demo Checklist (1 Minute Before Evaluator Arrives)
1. Open terminal in the project directory:
   ```cmd
   cd "Course Recommonder  FLEXI CA3"
   python app.py
   ```
2. Open browser at `http://127.0.0.1:7860`.
3. Verify that the UI loads with the dark glassmorphism theme and 5 tabs visible.

---

## Step-by-Step Demo Walkthrough

### Step 1: Initial Hook & System Overview (30 Seconds)
- **What to Say**:
  > *"Respected professors, I am presenting **EduAgent**, a Multi-Agent AI Course Recommender and Curriculum Orchestrator. Unlike standard platforms that merely show a list of courses, EduAgent coordinates four specialized AI agents to diagnose skill gaps, search our 100-course catalog using hybrid TF-IDF retrieval, build a sequenced 12-week roadmap, and conduct an academic feasibility audit."*

---

### Step 2: Tab 1 — Triggering the Multi-Agent Pipeline (60 Seconds)
- **What to Do**:
  1. Click the preset button: `🤖 Aspiring AI`.
  2. Notice how the form auto-populates:
     - Target Role: `AI & Machine Learning Engineer`
     - Experience: `Beginner`
     - Weekly Commitment: `12 hrs/week`
     - Learning Style: `Hands-on Project Oriented`
  3. Click the prominent purple button: **`🚀 Orchestrate Course Recommendations`**.
- **What to Say while it loads (instant in offline mode or ~2s with API)**:
  > *"When I click 'Orchestrate', our central MultiAgentOrchestrator initiates a sequential 4-agent pipeline.
  > First, the **ProfilerAgent** deconstructs my background to identify key skill gaps like Linear Algebra, Supervised Learning, and PyTorch.
  > Next, the **RetrievalAgent** runs our hybrid algorithm—fusing TF-IDF cosine similarity, ratings, and level alignment—to pull the top candidate courses from Harvard, Stanford, and DeepLearning.AI.
  > Notice the **Agent Insight** box on each card explaining exactly why that course was chosen for this student's profile."*
- **What to Point Out on Screen**:
  - Point to the **Diagnostic Banner** at the top showing *Current: Beginner $\rightarrow$ Target: Intermediate/Advanced*.
  - Point to the **Match Percentage** pill (e.g., *90% Match*).
  - Point to the **Explore Course ↗** button linking directly to the real course URL.

---

### Step 3: Tab 2 — The Career Learning Roadmap (45 Seconds)
- **What to Do**:
  1. Click on **Tab 2: 🗺️ Career Learning Roadmap**.
- **What to Say**:
  > *"Now we move to Tab 2. This is generated by our third agent, the **PathPlannerAgent**. Instead of leaving the student confused about which course to take first, it sequences them into four logical phases:
  > - **Phase 1**: Foundations & Syntax (Weeks 1-3)
  > - **Phase 2**: Applied Frameworks & Architecture (Weeks 4-7)
  > - **Phase 3**: Advanced Engineering (Weeks 8-10)
  > - **Phase 4**: Capstone Project & Portfolio Deployment (Weeks 11-12)
  > Notice the **Milestone Deliverable** on each card and the customized **Capstone Portfolio Project** blueprint at the bottom."*
- **What to Point Out on Screen**:
  - Point to the top metric cards: *Total Duration: 12 Weeks*, *Critic Feasibility Score: 88%*, *Total Study: 120 hrs*.
  - Point to the **Pedagogical Auditor Verdict** generated by our fourth agent, the **CriticAgent**, which highlights potential bottlenecks and study tips.

---

### Step 4: Tab 4 — Proof of Multi-Agent Execution (30 Seconds)
- **What to Do**:
  1. Click on **Tab 4: 🤖 Agent Architecture & Trace**.
- **What to Say**:
  > *"To verify that this is a true multi-agent system and not just a single static prompt, Tab 4 shows our real-time **Execution Trace Table**.
  > Here the evaluators can inspect each agent's individual execution timestamp, action description, and latency in seconds.
  > Below it, we provide the complete raw state JSON payload containing all intermediate outputs passed between agents."*
- **What to Point Out on Screen**:
  - Highlight the rows in the table: `ProfilerAgent`, `RetrievalAgent`, `PathPlannerAgent`, `CriticAgent`.

---

### Step 5: Tab 3 & 5 — Dataset & Technical Resilience (15 Seconds)
- **What to Do**:
  1. Click on **Tab 3: 📊 Course Catalog Explorer** $\rightarrow$ type `"Python"` in the search box to show live table filtering across the 100-course catalog.
  2. Click on **Tab 5: ⚙️ Settings & API Config**.
- **What to Say**:
  > *"Finally, Tab 3 allows filtering our curated catalog of 100 courses. In Tab 5, evaluators can see that EduAgent supports cloud LLMs including Groq Llama 3.3, OpenAI, and Google Gemini, while also featuring an intelligent offline heuristic engine so the system never fails during evaluations."*

---

### Step 6: Concluding Sentence (Ready for Viva)
- **What to Say**:
  > *"This concludes the live demonstration of EduAgent. Thank you, professors. I am ready to answer any questions about the algorithms, multi-agent architecture, or implementation details."*
